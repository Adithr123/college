Mission statement

Understanding the true cost of recurring subscriptions is essential for high school seniors preparing for college and beyond. Small monthly charges accumulate quickly, hidden fees compound the burden, and annual price increases multiply the impact over time. This tool visualizes how subscriptions really drain your wallet—not through their sticker price, but through the relentless repetition and hidden costs that sneak into your account. Make informed decisions about which subscriptions are worth it.
