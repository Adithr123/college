I'm a freshman at UC Riverside studying Chemistry on a pre-med track, working toward becoming a pediatrician. The most impactful healthcare happens when clinical skill meets genuine empathy: recognizing when someone needs help before they ask.

That belief shows up in how I work: co-founding a tutoring club that served over 200 students, volunteering at community events, and serving as a certified lifeguard and BLS provider.

Community philosophy

At a community event I noticed Spanish-speaking families struggling, so I added Spanish translations to every sign in my section. Good leadership means noticing who is left out and removing barriers before anyone has to ask.
